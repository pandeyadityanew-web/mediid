Patient-Controlled Medical Identity & Clinical Governance

During acute medical crises, healthcare professionals face an immediate information vacuum: blood types, life-threatening drug allergies, and active medications are often unknown. Naive solutions print medical data on public QR cards, destroying patient privacy.

SahayID bridges this gap through a cryptographic zero-knowledge architecture. Physical QR badges store strictly an unguessable routing URL. Normal doctor inquiries require active patient consent and OTP validation, while emergency responders use a time-limited, monitored break-glass gateway.

Academic Prototype Notice: SahayID is engineered as a research and demonstration prototype for secure healthcare identity access, consent models, and clinical auditability.

Zero-Knowledge Routing

Physical QR cards encode solely the gateway link. Zero medical data is embedded in the physical card payload.

Consent + OTP Verification

Doctors must request access; patients approve on their dashboard and share a 6-digit one-time passcode to authorize review.

Emergency Break-Glass

Paramedics in life-threatening scenarios can access critical vitals only, backed by mandatory responder declaration and audit logs.

Bilateral Audit Trail

Every doctor access, consent event, and emergency break-glass is immutably logged with timestamp, identity, and network IP.

Workflow & Lifecycle

How SahayID Works

A five-stage lifecycle balancing patient sovereignty with emergency readiness.

1

Create Your SahayID

Register securely with your basic credentials to receive your unique collision-resistant SahayID Number (MED-XXXXXXXX).

2

Add Medical Information

Populate your medical profile with blood group, allergies, chronic conditions, active prescriptions, surgical history, and next-of-kin contacts.

3

Get Your Secure QR Badge

Download your scannable QR card or print your physical wallet badge. The QR contains solely routing parameters—zero health data.

4

Authorized Clinical Access (Consent + OTP)

When visiting a clinic or hospital, your doctor scans your QR or enters your SahayID. You approve the request on your dashboard and provide the 6-digit OTP to grant temporary access.

5

Emergency Break-Glass When Needed

If incapacitated during an emergency, first responders use the audited break-glass portal to view essential vitals and contacts only, while private records remain protected.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding patient privacy, doctor verification, and emergency break-glass triage.

SahayID is a privacy-first digital healthcare identity infrastructure. Unlike static medical cards that print sensitive medical diagnoses directly in public view, SahayID issues a cryptographic identifier (MED-XXXXXXXX) and zero-knowledge QR routing badge.

Your medical conditions, prescriptions, and surgical history are encrypted at rest and can only be unlocked with your explicit one-time passcode (OTP) or through an audited emergency break-glass protocol.

When you visit a clinic or hospital, your doctor searches your SahayID Number or scans your QR code. This generates a secure consent request on your Patient Dashboard.

When you approve the request, the platform produces a temporary 6-digit verification OTP (valid for 5 minutes). Once your doctor enters this code, they are granted read-only access to your clinical record for a 30-minute consultation window.

In acute, life-threatening crises where a patient is unconscious or unable to provide an OTP, emergency responders and trauma teams can initiate a monitored Break-Glass protocol.

The responder must declare their identity, organization, and clinical justification.
