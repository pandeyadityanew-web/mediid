CRITICAL MEDICAL ACCESS

Your Medical Identity. Available When It Matters.

SahayID is a privacy-first digital medical identity system that allows patients to securely store essential emergency clinical information and provide controlled access to verified doctors and first responders when every second counts.

Create Your SahayID Patient Login Doctor Portal

Why SahayID?

Engineered for patient privacy, emergency speed, and clinical trust.

Secure Role-Based Access

Patient medical information is protected through server-side role authentication, separating patients and verified doctors.

Emergency Ready

A QR-based SahayID Number allows first responders and doctors to initiate controlled clinical access through the emergency gateway.

Privacy First

Medical information is never stored directly inside the QR code. All requests require authorization and leave a permanent audit trail.

How It Works

Seven straightforward steps from identity creation to verified clinical access.

1

Create your SahayID

Register with your name to receive your unique SahayID Number.

2

Add medical information

Enter blood group, critical allergies, and medications.

3

Generate your QR

Obtain a scannable digital badge for wallet or lockscreen.

4

Responder scans QR

First responders or doctors scan to open the emergency gateway.

5

Verify emergency purpose

Responders state identity and confirm medical purpose.

6

Temporary access granted

10-minute temporary cryptographic token issued.

7

Access is logged

Audit entry recorded in the patient's dashboard.
