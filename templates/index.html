Privacy-First Digital Health ID

Your Medical Identity. Available When It Matters.

MediID is a privacy-focused medical identity system that allows patients to securely store essential emergency medical information and provide controlled access when needed.

Built for Healthcare Privacy & Emergency Access

Every design decision prioritizes patient confidentiality and emergency readiness.

Secure

Patient medical information is protected through authentication and controlled access.

Emergency Ready

A QR-based MediID allows emergency responders to initiate emergency access.

Privacy First

Medical information is never stored directly inside the QR code.

How It Works

Seven straightforward steps from identity creation to verified emergency access.

1

Create your MediID

Register with your name and emergency credentials.

2

Add medical information

Enter blood group, critical allergies, and medications.

3

Generate your QR

Obtain a scannable medical ID for lockscreen or print.

4

Responder scans QR

First responders scan to open the emergency gateway.

5

Verify emergency purpose

Responders state identity and confirm medical purpose.

6

Temporary access granted

10-minute temporary cryptographic token issued.

7

Access is logged

Audit entry recorded in the patient's dashboard.
